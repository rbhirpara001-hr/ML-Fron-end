Key Capabilities of the System

Multi-Factor Assessment

Evaluates 11 vital patient features including age, gender, BMI, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, and lifestyle factors (smoking, alcohol, physical activity).

Machine Learning Engine

Utilizes a trained Logistic Regression classification model combined with StandardScaler feature transformation, providing calibrated probability estimates and robust risk categorization.

Actionable Guidance

Generates clinical recommendations tailored to the patient's blood pressure stage (AHA criteria), BMI classification, and specific metabolic and lifestyle risk indicators.
